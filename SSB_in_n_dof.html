Vacuum Manifold in 3D Field Space [O(3) → O(2)]

Visualizing Goldstone Modes:
This plot shows the 3-dimensional field space (φ₁, φ₂, φ₃), not physical space.
  • If μ² ≥ 0: The vacuum is a single point at the origin (Unbroken O(3) symmetry). All 3 fields have mass.
  • If μ² < 0: The vacuum expands into a spherical shell (Broken symmetry). The system chooses a single point on this shell.
  • Moving radially (perpendicular to the shell) costs potential energy: This is the massive σ boson.
  • Moving tangentially (along the surface of the shell) costs no potential energy: These are the two massless Goldstone bosons (π₁, π₂).
