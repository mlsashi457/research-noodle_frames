Standard Model Higgs Branching Ratios

Kinematic Thresholds to observe:
- ~125 GeV: The "sweet spot". The Higgs is light enough that the $b\bar{b}$ channel is dominant, but heavy enough that loop decays ($\gamma\gamma$, $gg$) and off-shell vector bosons ($WW^*$, $ZZ^*$) are highly visible.
- ~160 GeV: The $WW$ threshold ($2 M_W$). The branching ratio to $W$ bosons approaches 90%.
- ~350 GeV: The $t\bar{t}$ threshold ($2 m_t$). The top quark decay channel opens up, taking a significant share of the decays at very high masses.
