Simulation Interpretation Guide

This simulator models the probability space of Higgs boson decay channels using statistical Monte Carlo event simulation.

How the Monte Carlo Simulation works:

  • Click 🌧️ Rain Decays to continuously generate random points across the Sample Space \(S\). Each point represents a single simulated Higgs boson decay.
  • Click ⚡ Single Event to generate one decay event at a time and visualize its impact on the counters.
  • The simulator checks where each point lands and increments the respective counters: \(N_S, N_A, N_B, N_{A \cap B}, N_{A \cup B}\).
  • The simulated probability is calculated as the ratio of counts: \(P_{\text{sim}}(E) = \frac{N_{\text{region}}}{N_{\text{total}}}\).

Real-time Indicators & Color Codes

  • Cyan dots fall strictly inside \(A\) (\(H \to \gamma\gamma\)). They count towards \(N_A\) and \(N_{A \cup B}\).
  • Magenta dots fall strictly inside \(B\) (\(H \to ZZ^*\)). They count towards \(N_B\) and \(N_{A \cup B}\).
  • Indigo dots fall in the overlap region \(A \cap B\). They count towards \(N_A\), \(N_B\), \(N_{A \cap B}\), and \(N_{A \cup B}\).
  • Slate dots represent all other decay channels of the Higgs Boson in the Sample Space \(S\).

Convergence (Law of Large Numbers): The chart shows both Theoretical and Simulated percentages side by side. As you let the "decay rain" run, the emerald simulated bars will dynamically adjust and align with the theoretical values.
