Histogram to Continuous p.d.f.

Observe how a discrete empirical histogram of a random variable smooths into a continuous probability density function \( f(x) \) as the number of events \( N \to \infty \) and the bin width \( \Delta x \to 0 \).

DENSITY NORMALIZATION:
\( \text{Density} = \frac{\text{Counts per bin}}{N \cdot \Delta x} \)
